### Company

- **Shandong company fined for 4 years of financial fraud and fraudulent IPO despite withdrawal** · covered by 2 outlets
 - Yuweng Information (渔翁信息; a Chinese information technology firm) fabricated total revenue of **37.5885 million yuan** from 2020 to the first half of 2023, according to the Jiangxi Securities Regulatory Bureau. [National Business Daily, 2026-07-30](http://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-07-30/4526064.html).
 - The Jiangxi Securities Regulatory Bureau fined Yuweng Information **4 million yuan** for fraudulent issuance. [National Business Daily, 2026-07-30](http://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-07-30/4526064.html).
 - Actual controllers Guo Gang and Liu Guihua were each fined **4 million yuan**, and total fines on the company and related persons amount to **17.9 million yuan**. [National Business Daily, 2026-07-30](http://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-07-30/4526064.html).
 - The company voluntarily withdrew its IPO application in 2024 but was still penalized, with regulatory focus on financial fraud and concealed equity holdings. [National Business Daily, 2026-07-30](http://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-07-30/4526064.html).

 - IPO proceeds will be fully used for **technology research and capacity expansion**. [Securities Times, 2026-07-30](http://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4046444.html).

 - Cambricon (寒武纪; a Chinese AI-chip designer) plans to grant **5 million** restricted shares at **750 yuan** per share as an incentive plan, with the initial grant of 4 million shares covering **945 employees** (85.37% of total staff), according to Cambricon. - **SAMR seeks public comment on frozen beverage production permit review rules** · covered by 3 outlets
 - The State Administration for Market Regulation (市场监管总局; China's market regulator) published a draft for public comment on the 'Detailed Rules for Production License Review of Frozen Beverages' with a **30-day** comment period starting July 29, 2026. [industry.caijing.com.cn, 2026-07-29](http://industry.caijing.com.cn/20260729/5174195.shtml).
 - The draft adds new frozen beverage types beyond the six common categories and explicitly prohibits production by repackaging. [industry.caijing.com.cn, 2026-07-29](http://industry.caijing.com.cn/20260729/5174195.shtml).
 - It refines facility requirements, including dedicated cold storage with temperature overshoot alarms, and mandates a full-chain food safety management system. [industry.caijing.com.cn, 2026-07-29](http://industry.caijing.com.cn/20260729/5174195.shtml).

- **SAMR issues national standard on smart home appliance quality safety risks** · covered by 3 outlets
 - The State Administration for Market Regulation approved and released a national standard for quality safety risk classification and evaluation of smart home appliances, effective **February 1, 2027**. [m.21jingji.com, 2026-07-29](http://m.21jingji.com/article/20260729/herald/c580683d1e2b65a10b762c464a98fa34.html).
 - The standard classifies risks into four categories: **electrical safety, functional safety, information security, and data and privacy protection**. [m.21jingji.com, 2026-07-29](http://m.21jingji.com/article/20260729/herald/c580683d1e2b65a10b762c464a98fa34.html).
 - It establishes a comprehensive evaluation framework integrating product safety attributes, industry regulatory compliance data, and consumer usage feedback, with risk levels A, B, C, and D. [m.21jingji.com, 2026-07-29](http://m.21jingji.com/article/20260729/herald/c580683d1e2b65a10b762c464a98fa34.html).
