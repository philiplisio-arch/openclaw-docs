### Trade

- **US Imposes New Tariffs of 10-12.5% on Chinese Goods** · covered by 5 outlets

[National Business Daily, 2026-07-24](http://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-07-24/4505263.html).

- **Greater Bay Area's Nine Mainland Cities See H1 Trade Up 20.9% YoY** · covered by 2 outlets
 - In the first half of 2026, the total import and export value of nine cities in the Greater Bay Area reached **5.3 trillion yuan**, up **20.9%** year-on-year, outpacing the national average by 4 percentage points and contributing 24.9% to national growth. ### Geopolitics

- **China Adds 14 EU Entities to Export Control List** · covered by 2 outlets
 - The Ministry of Commerce (商务部) placed **14 EU entities**, including Rafat Group, on the export control list, prohibiting the export of dual-use items to them; ongoing activities must cease immediately. [Securities Times, 2026-07-24](http://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4040044.html).
 - The decision, announced on **24 July 2026**, is a direct retaliation for EU sanctions on 14 Chinese companies and takes effect immediately under the Export Control Law and the Regulations on the Export Control of Dual-Use Items. [People's Daily, 2026-07-24](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0724/c1004-40767512.html).
 - The list includes Lafert S.p.A. and Rheinmetall AG, among others. [www.gelonghui.com, 2026-07-24](http://www.gelonghui.com/p/5736377).

- **PBOC to Conduct Over 2 Trillion Yuan Overnight Reverse Repo Operations** · covered by 10 outlets
 - The People’s Bank of China (PBOC) announced overnight reverse repo operations on **29-31 July and 3 August**, with daily amounts of 600 billion yuan for the first three days and 300 billion yuan on 3 August, totalling over **2 trillion yuan**. [Securities Times, 2026-07-24](http://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4040963.html).
 - The operations aim to smooth month-end liquidity fluctuations, while the 7-day reverse repo rate remains the main policy rate, according to PBOC Deputy Governor Zou Lan (邹澜). [Securities Times, 2026-07-24](http://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4040963.html).
 - The PBOC also conducted a **500 billion yuan** Medium-term Lending Facility (MLF) operation on 24 July, and when combined with 700 billion yuan in buyout reverse repos, total mid-term liquidity operations added **800 billion yuan**, reversing a four-month contraction. [Jiemian, 2026-07-24](http://www.jiemian.com/article/14819074.html).
