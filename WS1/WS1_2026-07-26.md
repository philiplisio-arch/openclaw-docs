### Macro

- **China's H1 2026 GDP and key economic indicators show steady recovery** · covered by 3 outlets
 - In the first half of 2026, China's general public budget revenue reached **12.1 trillion yuan**, up 4.7% year-on-year, completing 54.8% of the annual budget, according to the Ministry of Finance (财政部). [Jiemian, 2026-07-26](http://www.jiemian.com/article/14813873.html).
 - General public budget expenditure was **14.33 trillion yuan**, up 1.5% year-on-year, completing 47.8% of the annual budget. [Jiemian, 2026-07-26](http://www.jiemian.com/article/14813873.html).
 - Securities stamp duty increased by **97.3%** year-on-year in the first half. [Jiemian, 2026-07-26](http://www.jiemian.com/article/14813873.html).
 - The broader fiscal deficit (including government funds) narrowed by **679.3 billion yuan** year-on-year to 4.57 trillion yuan. [Jiemian, 2026-07-26](http://www.jiemian.com/article/14813873.html).

- **China's water conservancy investment reaches 515.1 billion yuan in H1 2026** · covered by 4 outlets
 - In the first half of the year, national water conservancy construction investment reached **515.1 billion yuan**, according to the Ministry of Water Resources (水利部). [finance.china.com.cn, 2026-07-25](http://finance.china.com.cn/news/20260725/6317731.shtml).
 - Water conservancy infrastructure construction absorbed **1.344 million** jobs and paid **20.99 billion yuan** in wages. [finance.china.com.cn, 2026-07-25](http://finance.china.com.cn/news/20260725/6317731.shtml).
 - Nationwide, **33,000 water projects** were implemented, with **25 new major projects** started, totaling 264.6 billion yuan in investment. [finance.china.com.cn, 2026-07-25](http://finance.china.com.cn/news/20260725/6317731.shtml).
 - A total of **258 billion yuan** was secured from local government special bonds, bank loans, and social capital, up 7% year-on-year, including **10.84 billion yuan** from private capital, up 85.8%. [finance.china.com.cn, 2026-07-25](http://finance.china.com.cn/news/20260725/6317731.shtml).

### Company

- **SAMR fines Ctrip for monopolistic practices, orders comprehensive rectification** · covered by 2 outlets
 - SAMR imposed a total penalty of **5.179 billion yuan** on Ctrip Group (携程; a Chinese online travel agency, also known as Trip.com Group) for abusing market dominance, according to the State Administration for Market Regulation (市场监管总局). [People's Daily, 2026-07-26](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0726/c1004-40767917.html).
 - Ctrip is ordered to refund **122 million yuan** of reserved deposits forcibly deducted from hotel operators. [People's Daily, 2026-07-26](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0726/c1004-40767917.html).
 - Illegal gains of **1.658 billion yuan** were confiscated, and Ctrip was fined **3.521 billion yuan**, representing **7.5%** of its 2025 sales of **46.958 billion yuan** in China. [People's Daily, 2026-07-26](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0726/c1004-40767917.html).
 - Ctrip abused its dominant position by requiring exclusive cooperation from 'special brand' hotels and forcing 'gold' and 'no brand' hotels to offer the lowest prices across platforms. [finance.sina.com.cn, 2026-07-25](http://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/zqgd/2026-07-25/doc-iniiytzh9154807.shtml).

- **ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) to list on Monday; market speculates on performance** · covered by 2 outlets
 - Changxin Technology (长鑫科技; a Chinese DRAM chipmaker) will list on the STAR Market on **July 27, 2026**, at an issue price of **8.66 yuan per share**. [Securities Times, 2026-07-26](http://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4038431.html).
 - The initial fundraising of about **57.9 billion yuan** is the largest IPO on the STAR Market, breaking the previous record of **53.2 billion yuan** by SMIC. [Securities Times, 2026-07-26](http://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4038431.html).
 - In the first quarter of 2026, the company achieved revenue of **50.8 billion yuan**, up **719.13%** year-on-year, and net profit of **33.012 billion yuan**, up **1268.45%**, according to the prospectus. [Securities Times, 2026-07-26](http://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4038431.html).
 - The price-to-earnings ratio based on the issue price is **278.03 times**. [Securities Times, 2026-07-26](http://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4038431.html).

- **CATL posts 43.3 billion yuan H1 net profit, up 42%; launches record A-share buyback of up to 40 billion yuan** · covered by 9 outlets
 - CATL (宁德时代; a Chinese battery giant) reported H1 2026 net profit attributable to shareholders of **43.284 billion yuan**, up **41.98%** year-on-year, and revenue of **276.917 billion yuan**, up **54.80%** year-on-year, according to the company. [National Business Daily, 2026-07-24](http://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-07-24/4511531.html).
 - CATL announced a share buyback program of **20 billion to 40 billion yuan** at a maximum price of **573 yuan per share**; the repurchased shares will be canceled to reduce registered capital. [National Business Daily, 2026-07-24](http://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-07-24/4511531.html).
 - This buyback plan is the largest in A-share history, exceeding Gree's previous **15 billion yuan** plan. [National Business Daily, 2026-07-24](http://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-07-24/4511531.html).
 - Separately, Quanjude (全聚德; a Chinese restaurant chain) expects H1 2026 net profit of **12.8 million to 15.5 million yuan**, up 3.39% to 25.20% year-on-year, attributed to improved cost control and store optimization. [industry.caijing.com.cn, 2026-07-24](http://industry.caijing.com.cn/20260724/5173341.shtml).

- **Court rules against bank refusing to honor 27-year-old passbook deposit** · covered by 1 outlet
 - A court in Henan ordered a bank in Minquan County to repay the principal of **17,900 yuan** with interest at the current demand deposit rate from August 24, 2004, after the bank refused to honor a 27-year-old deposit, according to the People's Court of Minquan County. [m.21jingji.com, 2026-07-25](http://m.21jingji.com/article/20260725/herald/4095756a0f2a9bb16c075418cc9c05d4.html).
 - The appeal court upheld the ruling, stating that system data migration and incomplete retention are operational risks that should not be borne by the depositor, according to the Shangqiu Intermediate People's Court. [m.21jingji.com, 2026-07-25](http://m.21jingji.com/article/20260725/herald/4095756a0f2a9bb16c075418cc9c05d4.html).

- **Rockwell Automation to attend China International Import Expo for sixth consecutive year; stock edges up** · covered by 1 outlet
 - Rockwell Automation announced its sixth consecutive participation in the China International Import Expo, focusing on industrial AI and sustainable development, according to the company. [Economic Observer, 2026-07-26](http://www.eeo.com.cn/2026/0726/974055.shtml).
 - Its core approach is "AIOT" (deep integration of AI with IT/OT) to empower the full value chain. [Economic Observer, 2026-07-26](http://www.eeo.com.cn/2026/0726/974055.shtml).

### Policy

- **China allows online transfer of enterprise annuity personal accounts** · covered by 6 outlets
 - The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (人力资源社会保障部) has issued the "Interim Procedures for Transfer and Continuation of Enterprise Annuity Personal Accounts," effective **January 1, 2027**. [People's Daily, 2026-07-25](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0725/c1004-40767614.html).
 - Employees can apply for transfer through national unified online service channels such as the National Social Insurance Public Service Platform. [People's Daily, 2026-07-25](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0725/c1004-40767614.html).
 - Personal contributions, vested employer contributions, transferred occupational annuity, and corresponding investment earnings can all be transferred with the account, according to Xinhua. [m.21jingji.com, 2026-07-25](http://m.21jingji.com/article/20260725/herald/5b80f20b959c76c31dd8fb46e63ab03a.html).

- **China launches campaign to improve overseas antitrust compliance for enterprises** · covered by 2 outlets
 - The State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局) issued a notice to deploy a special action to enhance enterprises' overseas antitrust compliance capabilities, focusing on key scenarios such as overseas investment, cross-border M&A, and product exports. [People's Daily, 2026-07-25](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0725/c1004-40767616.html).
 - The action aims to achieve comprehensive coverage of overseas antitrust system publicity and significant improvement in enterprise compliance capabilities within about **one year**. [People's Daily, 2026-07-25](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0725/c1004-40767616.html).
 - One task is to revise the "Guidelines for Overseas Antitrust Compliance of Enterprises" and add model cases. [finance.china.com.cn, 2026-07-24](http://finance.china.com.cn/news/20260724/6317541.shtml).

- **China's 'six networks' to modernize industrial system: policy interpretation** · covered by 1 outlet
 - The "six networks" — water network, new power grid, computing power network, new generation communication network, urban underground pipeline network, and logistics network — are being deployed to modernize the industrial system. [People's Daily, 2026-07-26](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0726/c1004-40767914.html).
 - Weichai Lovol's (a Chinese agricultural machinery manufacturer) 'super factory' in Shandong produces a high-end intelligent tractor every **4 minutes** using 5G-A network and AI inspection. [People's Daily, 2026-07-26](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0726/c1004-40767914.html).
