### Energy

- **China's power load hits record high three times this summer** · covered by 2 outlets
 - The national electricity load hit a record high three times, reaching a maximum of **1.553 billion kW**, an increase of about 45 million kW compared to last year's peak, according to the National Energy Administration (国家能源局). [People's Daily, 2026-07-30](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0730/c1004-40771169.html).
 - As of end of June, total wind and solar installed capacity reached **19.51 billion kW**, accounting for about 48.3% of total installed capacity, according to the National Energy Administration. [Yicai, 2026-07-30](http://www.yicai.com/news/103299040.html).
 - The newly commissioned Shaanbei–Anhui UHV DC project brought the total number of operational UHV channels nationwide to **46**, with west-to-east power transmission capacity exceeding 340 million kW, according to the National Energy Administration. [People's Daily, 2026-07-30](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0730/c1004-40771169.html).
 - Coal reserves at national regulated power plants can sustain **more than 30 days** of consumption, a historically good level, according to the National Energy Administration. [People's Daily, 2026-07-30](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0730/c1004-40771169.html).
- **Experts analyze China's energy mix shift amid record power demand** · covered by 1 outlet
 - In the first half of the year, renewable energy generation exceeded **40%** of total generation for the first time, and coal power generation fell below **50%** for the first time, according to researcher Wei Qijia from the National Development and Reform Commission. [CCTV, 2026-07-31](http://news.cctv.com/2026/07/31/ARTID4BS281rTE22Fwfhd0cM260731.shtml).
