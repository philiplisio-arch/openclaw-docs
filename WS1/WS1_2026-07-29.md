### Policy

- **China to adjust urban land use tax for energy and resource companies** · covered by 2 outlets
 - The Ministry of Finance and the State Taxation Administration announced an adjustment to the urban land use tax policy for some energy and resource industry enterprises, according to the two ministries. [People’s Daily, 2026-07-28](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0728/c1004-40769674.html).
 - The preferential tax exemption for major production and operation land of enterprises in coal, mining, oil and gas, building materials, and electric power industries will be canceled. [People’s Daily, 2026-07-28](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0728/c1004-40769674.html).
 - The policy includes a transitional phase: from September 1, 2026 to August 31, 2027, tax is levied at half the amount due; from September 1, 2027, the full amount is levied. [People’s Daily, 2026-07-28](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0728/c1004-40769674.html).
